# Topic bank (broad appeal, natural endpoint)

Ladder: animals by size to the blue whale; fastest things ever made; loudest sounds ever; tallest buildings across history; biggest explosions; largest stars; heaviest things ever lifted; oldest living things; most expensive things ever sold; smallest things we can see.
Descent: ocean depth to the Challenger Deep; digging to Earth's core; up through the atmosphere to space; falling into Jupiter / the Sun; back in time to the Big Bang; zoom into a human cell; how far humans have travelled.
Story: what if the Moon disappeared; the first hour of a supervolcano; Earth after humans vanish; what if you fell into a black hole; a day on every planet; the Titanic's sinking minute by minute; what if the oceans drained.
Money series: billionaire wealth in $100 bills; per-second earnings; record contracts.

Rhythm: 1 Short a day (mostly Ladder/Descent); 1 long-form a week (Story or long Descent) cut into 3-4 Shorts that point to it.
Repeat what works: when an episode beats 90% viewed, make 3 more in the same family next week.
