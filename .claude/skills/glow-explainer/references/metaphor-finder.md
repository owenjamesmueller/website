# Metaphor finder: turn ANY topic into a picture with stakes

Both styles live or die on one physical metaphor that *is* the idea. Facts are forgettable; a person
sinking, climbing, carrying, leaking or lighting up is not. Use this for every video, on any topic:
money, relationships, discipline, fitness, school, career, mental health, social media, habits,
confidence, parenting, business, history, science.

## 1. Find the tension (write these 4 lines before anything else)
1. **The want**: what the viewer wants ("to be rich", "to be loved", "to be fit", "to be respected").
2. **The trap**: the thing that feels right but quietly costs them ("spending raises", "people-pleasing",
   "waiting for motivation", "scrolling to relax").
3. **The hidden mechanism**: why the trap works, in one sentence ("every raise comes with a bigger payment",
   "every yes to them is a no to you", "motivation shows up after you start").
4. **The counter-move**: the unpopular choice that wins ("own things that pay you", "say no", "start badly").

## 2. Translate the mechanism into physics
Ask: *if this idea were an object or a place, what would visibly get worse over time?* Pick the family
that matches the mechanism, then make it concrete, specific and a little absurd.

| mechanism | physical families (pick one, then invent a specific version) |
|---|---|
| something slowly drains you | leaking bucket/boat, battery draining, hourglass, candle burning down, phone at 1 %, balloon deflating, bleeding health bar |
| something grows if you're patient | seed → tree, snowball, compound staircase, plant in a pot, bricks becoming a house, interest as gold fruit |
| other people hold you back | chains/anchors, crabs in a bucket, hands pulling an ankle, rope tug-of-war, people standing on a ladder rung, a gift box with an extinguisher in it |
| you follow without thinking | blindfolded crowd, sheep, conveyor belt, escalator going down, a queue to a cliff, puppets on strings |
| two choices, different futures | forked road, two doors, split screen of the same morning, two jars, two buckets, mirror twins |
| effort that looks pointless now | climbing in fog, digging a tunnel, building a raft in a storm, planting in winter, laying track before the train |
| pressure building to a breaking point | dam cracking, kettle whistling, rising water, a stack of plates wobbling, a rubber band stretching |
| hidden cost behind something nice | a free cheese trap, a fishing hook with a reward on it, a golden cage, a beautiful room with no door |
| your environment shapes you | rooms with lamps (who's in your room), a fishbowl, a garden vs a desert, a plant moved to the sunlight |
| time slipping away | calendar pages flying, a clock face, sand pouring, a candle, the same day on loop |
| an identity/mask | a mask cracking, a costume, a mirror showing someone else, a puppet cutting strings |

Then add **stakes** (what's the worst that happens in-frame: sinking, falling, the light going out, the
door locking) and **a reward image** (light, gold, surfacing, a door opening, a tree, a view from the top).

## 4. Check it (both modes)
- Could someone **muted** understand it from the picture alone? (Mode B: must. Mode A: should.)
- Is something **at risk from the first frame**?
- Is there one **object you can follow** from start to end (the seed, the bucket, the chain, the battery)?
- Does the payoff image **echo the setup** (the same seed is now the tree, the same chain now broken)?
- Is it **original**? Variations on a genre are fine; never recreate a specific creator's video.
