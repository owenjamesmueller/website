Most people think you need money to get rich, but at eighteen you already have the one thing money can't buy back. Time. Imagine two eighteen year olds who both get their first job. The first one spends every paycheck, because he figures he'll start saving once he earns more. The second one puts away just two hundred dollars a month, and never touches it. By forty, he has put in fifty thousand dollars, but his account says a hundred and forty thousand, because his money has been earning money for twenty two years. The first one finally starts at thirty. He saves twice as much, and still ends up with half. But saving is only half of it. At eighteen you can afford to be bad at things, so the second one spends his free time learning a skill people actually pay for. His income goes up every year, and every raise goes into something he owns. That's the whole trick. Broke people earn money and spend it. Rich people earn money and buy things that keep earning. So the earlier you start owning instead of spending, the less you'll ever need to earn.
