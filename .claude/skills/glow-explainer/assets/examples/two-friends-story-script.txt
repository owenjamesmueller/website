Two best friends got the same job at eighteen. Twenty years later, only one of them is rich, and the crazy part is, they earned almost exactly the same money. On their first payday, Marcus went straight to the mall and bought new shoes. Jake put two hundred dollars into an index fund, and everyone laughed at him. And honestly, for years, it looked like Marcus was winning. He financed a car at nineteen, while Jake was still taking the bus. But here's what nobody saw. Every night after work, Jake was teaching himself to code. He was terrible at it for almost a year. Then one day, a stranger paid him five hundred dollars for a website. His income doubled, and he did the one thing almost nobody does. He didn't change how he lived. Marcus got raises too. But every raise came with a bigger car payment, a nicer apartment, a newer phone. That's the whole difference. Marcus got paid by his job. Jake kept buying things that paid him, even while he slept. Twenty years later, Marcus still needs every paycheck. Jake doesn't need his at all. And nobody noticed the moment Jake got rich. It happened on that very first payday, while everyone was laughing.
