# Script template (90-130 words, ~35-50 s at 165 wpm)

Write numbers in words exactly as spoken. Short sentences. One idea per sentence. No "in this video".

```
[HOOK]      {Subject} is worth {figure in words}. But you've never actually seen {figure-short}.
[PHYSICAL]  In hundred-dollar bills, that's {n} bundles. Stack them... and it's taller than {landmark}.
            {One more physical beat: weight / trucks needed / football fields covered.}
[PERSONAL]  If you earned the average salary, you'd need to work {years in words} years.
            You'd have to have started {era: before the pyramids / when dinosaurs...}.
            Or: in the time you've watched this, {subject} made {live counter}.
[ESCALATE]  With it you could buy {thing 1}. Or {thing 2}. Or every {absurd thing 3}... {n} times.
[TWIST]     But here's the crazy part: {one reframing fact, the red number}.
[LOOP]      {Line that leads straight back into the hook sentence.}
```

## Example (illustrative numbers - always re-research)

> One hundred billion dollars. Everyone says it, but nobody's seen it.
> In hundred-dollar bills, that's a billion bills. Stacked up, it's sixty-eight miles tall - past where space officially begins.
> Earning sixty thousand a year, you'd need one point six million years. You'd have started before humans learned to use fire.
> You could buy every NBA team. Then give every American three hundred dollars. And still have change.
> But here's the crazy part: at five percent interest, it makes thirteen thousand dollars... every single minute.
> So how big is one hundred billion dollars?
