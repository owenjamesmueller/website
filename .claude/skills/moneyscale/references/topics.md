# Topic bank (pick figures with a news hook this week first)

Evergreen series (each a recurring playlist):
- **Net worth, to scale** - the richest people, live figures from real-time lists.
- **Contract, to scale** - record sports/entertainment contracts, per game / per second.
- **Budget, to scale** - government line items (military, NASA, national debt), per citizen.
- **Fine, to scale** - corporate fines as minutes/hours of the company's revenue (best twists).
- **Company per second** - revenue/profit per second of big companies; the live corner counter.
- **Movie budget, to scale** - blockbuster budgets vs. what else it could buy.
- **Then vs now** - same item's price across decades, inflation-adjusted.
- **Lottery / prize pots, to scale** - jackpot vs. odds made physical.

Hook formulas that test well in the money-scale genre:
- "{figure}. What does that actually look like?"
- "{Person} makes ${x} every second. Watch."
- "This fine sounds huge. It's {n} minutes of their revenue."
- "If you saved $1,000 a day since {year}, you'd still be short."

Publishing rhythm: 1 Short daily; every Sunday cut the week's 5-7 into a 4-6 min long-form compilation (long-form carries most ad revenue - Shorts RPM is cents).
